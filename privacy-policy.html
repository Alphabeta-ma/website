Who we are & scope

This privacy policy covers the Android offline rental-planning app Abdou Carthage, package ma.abdoucarthage.abdou_carthage, provided by ABDOU CARTHAGE RENT A CAR. The company’s details are listed below. This policy describes the reviewed app version 1.0.5; it does not describe a separate rental contract, an external email service, or Google Play’s own data processing. The interface is English-only, including on devices using other system languages.

02

Information the app handles

The app processes your search text, example-vehicle selections, rental dates, optional-extra selections, calculator values, favorites, checklist progress, theme choices locally to display the requested results. It does not ask for your name, identity document, driving licence, payment details or contact details. The developer does not receive, collect or share these app inputs, device identifiers, location, usage analytics or crash reports. The app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs and makes no application network requests.

03

Temporary memory & deletion

User choices are held in application memory only. The app does not write these choices to a database, preferences file, cloud backup or developer server. They disappear when the operating system terminates the app process; moving the app into the background or dismissing a screen does not necessarily terminate it. Use Clear session choices on the Company screen to reset the session’s inputs, plans, favorites, checklist and preferences. There are no user accounts or server records to delete.

04

Permissions, devices & security

The Android release declares no app permissions, including no INTERNET permission. It does not request access to location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, storage, notifications or advertising identifiers. Local processing reduces exposure to network services, but the app does not promise absolute security. Your operating system, keyboard, accessibility services and Google Play may process information under their own settings and policies, independently of this app. No claim is made that all device activity or all system services are private.

05

Sharing & service providers

The app sends no user data to the company, advertisers, analytics providers or AI services. Artwork is generated during development and included as static local files; there is no AI generation feature in the installed app and user inputs are not sent to an AI model. No in-app payments, reservations, messaging or account registration are provided.

07

Intended audience

The app is designed for adults planning a vehicle rental. It is not directed at children and does not request age, birth date or identity verification. It does not provide an age-verification service or establish eligibility to rent a vehicle; the agency confirms rental requirements separately.

08

Illustrations & rental estimates

Car images are AI-generated illustrations of generic example vehicles, not photographs of actual rental inventory. Vehicle names, example rates and extras are illustrative. Estimates do not include unspecified taxes, deposits, insurance or mileage terms and are not quotations or confirmed reservations. Confirm actual availability, prices and terms directly with the agency. Do not submit personal or payment information through this companion.
